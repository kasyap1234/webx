# Sponsor webx

webx is an independent, open-source web-data layer — self-hosted search,
scrape, crawl, extraction, and verified answers for agents. It's developed
and self-funded by one maintainer; sponsorship keeps it that way.

## Why sponsor

If webx is in your stack (or your agent's), sponsorship buys you a maintainer
who answers. Sponsor tiers are about *attention and influence*, not gratitude:

| Tier | What it buys |
|---|---|
| **$25/mo — Supporter** | Name in SPONSORS.md, sponsor badge on your profile |
| **$75/mo — User** | Priority issue triage — your bugs jump the queue |
| **$250/mo — Team** | Feature voting + roadmap input; a monthly maintainer call |
| **$1k/mo — Company** | Logo in README, integration help, design review of your webx deployment |
| **Custom** | Something else (bounty a feature, fund a provider, sponsored eval runs) — open an issue |

## What sponsors fund

Provider integrations, the eval corpora (`webx eval` — real Bing-click queries
aren't free to gather), benchmark infrastructure, and maintainer time on
correctness work like the citation verifier.

## Companies that depend on webx in production

Talk to me about a **license** instead — see README § Pricing. Sponsorship is
goodwill; production use is a license.

*One maintainer's honest note: sponsorship at this scale pays for coffee and
API keys, not a salary — but it does decide which features get built first.*
